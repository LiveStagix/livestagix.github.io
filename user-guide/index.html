01 / First run

Getting started

LiveStagix uses a preparation step to create media that is ready for the live wallpaper engine. Start with one photo or video from your device.

Choose your media

Tap Choose Media. Select Photos & videos to use the Android photo picker, or choose Browse device files to open folders and document providers. Web Wallpaper Search is another way to find online media; it requires an internet connection.

Choose the controls for that media type

Photos can use the Motion controls. Videos use Enhancements. The two panels are labelled Photo and Video in Controls, and unavailable controls are disabled for the other media type.

Open LiveStage and frame the media

Tap LiveStage at the top of Controls. Reposition and frame the image or video in the preview. Use Orientation for original media when a rotation is needed.

Prepare and apply

Tap Prepare and let preparation finish. Then tap Set as Wallpaper, choose Home, Lock or Both when the choices are offered, and complete Android's wallpaper preview and confirmation.

Already prepared this media? Open Prepared Media from Controls to reuse a prepared version instead of preparing the source again.

02 / Photos only

Photo Motion

Motion responds to the phone's movement and is available for photo wallpapers. Open the Motion card in Controls, then choose the movement style you want to use. Try one mode at a time while learning how each one feels.

2D Motion

Pan across the image

Moves the photo horizontally and vertically as the phone tilts. Sensitivity controls how strongly the image responds on the X and Y axes. Axis inversion reverses the corresponding direction.

3D Motion

Tilt and depth

Adds perspective movement through Roll, Pitch and Depth. Each axis has its own sensitivity and recovery behaviour. Speed limits help control how quickly the effect moves.

Rotation

Z-axis movement

Adds a separate rotational effect. Its sensitivity, recovery and rotation speed limit can be adjusted independently of 2D and 3D movement.

More Motion Settings

Safety Zoom Response (Kzoom)

Controls the additional zoom response used to help keep the wallpaper frame covered while motion is active. Set the slider to OFF to disable this response, or adjust it up to 1.00x.

Understanding the motion settings

Expand a motion mode to reveal its settings. The settings are organised into Sensitivity, Stabilisation and Advanced groups. Open a group to adjust it; tap outside the expanded group to move back one level, then tap outside the Motion card to close it after returning to the main rows.

  • Sensitivity: controls how much the image responds to phone movement. 2D has separate horizontal and vertical sensitivity; 3D separates Roll, Pitch and Depth.
  • Stabilisation: controls how motion settles back toward its resting position. Recovery controls are available for the relevant axes.
  • Advanced: provides additional rate and speed controls. 3D includes depth and zoom recovery options; Rotation has its own Z-axis speed limit.

More Motion Settings

Open More Motion Settings for the Safety Zoom Response (Kzoom) slider, Quick Presets and Reset to Defaults. Kzoom ranges from OFF (0) to 1.00x; OFF disables the extra zoom response. Quick Presets combine sensitivity levels from 1× to 4× with Gentle, Balanced or Fast stabilisation. Use a preset as a starting point, then fine-tune individual controls.

Motion is for photos. Video playback does not use these photo motion controls. Stronger motion may need more frame coverage; adjust Kzoom to suit the effect, or set it to OFF when you do not want the additional zoom response.

03 / Videos only

Video Enhancements

The Enhancements card in Controls contains video playback options. Their actual activity depends on the video, display, supported processing path and available device performance.

Frame Interpolation

Smoother movement

Creates intermediate frames between source frames to make eligible video movement appear smoother. Turn it on and adjust Interpolation Strength to control the effect.

Smart Upscale

Video scaling and sharpening

Select Auto to let the device profile choose a suitable path, or choose an available method such as Snapdragon GSR (SGSR), AMD RCAS or Linear Sharpener.

Performance vs Quality

The Smart Upscale slider balances processing preference against image quality. Moving toward Performance favours a lighter path; moving toward Quality favours stronger processing. A more demanding option is not automatically better on every device.

Enabled does not always mean active. A feature can remain enabled while the current video or device conditions make it ineligible to process. Check its active status and try Auto or a lighter setting when playback is not smooth.

04 / LiveStage

Crop & Frame

Use the crop/framing tool on LiveStage to position the media within the wallpaper preview. This is useful when the source image or video has a different shape from the phone screen.

  • Reposition: drag the media in the preview to move the visible area into the composition you want.
  • Zoom: change the media scale. Zooming out can reveal space around the source media.
  • Background: when zoomed out, choose a blurred background or a solid colour fill.
  • Blur: when zoomed out with Background set to Blur, adjust how strongly the enlarged background is blurred.
  • Brightness: with the same zoomed-out Blur background, adjust the background brightness.
  • Feather: soften the transition at the edge of the main media when using the zoomed-out background. A value of zero keeps the edge sharp.

Zoom, Blur, Brightness and Feather open one adjustment slider at a time. Tap the selected control again to close its slider. Some controls remain unavailable until their required zoom/background conditions are met.

Preview and movement tools

The phone-shaped preview button simulates the wallpaper with home-screen elements such as icons and a clock. For photos, the compass can simulate a handheld tilt, while the gauge can show live motion and scale readings. These are preview aids; they do not change the original media file.

Orientation

Use the Orientation control on LiveStage to choose Original, 90°, 180° or 270° rotation for original media. The control is hidden while viewing prepared media.

05 / Photo & video

Media Tuner

Open Media Tuner from the sliders icon on the LiveStage toolbar when a prepared wallpaper is ready. It adjusts the current prepared media, not the original source file.

Light

Brightness, Contrast, Highlights and Shadows adjust overall light and tonal balance.

Color

Saturation and Temperature adjust colour intensity and the warmer or cooler appearance.

Details

Denoise reduces noise; Structure adjusts local texture; Sharpness enhances edge definition.

Before / After

Press and hold Before to compare the unadjusted preview with your current tuning.

Use Reset to return the tuning controls to their default values. Applying your edits creates a separate Tuned prepared version; the base prepared version remains available. Applying a tune does not automatically replace the wallpaper already active in Android. Set the tuned version as wallpaper when you are ready.

06 / Android setup

Apply a wallpaper

  1. On LiveStage, make sure the intended media is selected and preparation has finished.
  2. Tap Set as Wallpaper. Choose Home, Lock or Both when those choices are shown.
  3. Android opens its wallpaper preview/confirmation flow. Review the preview and confirm the destination in the system interface.
  4. Return to LiveStagix and allow the app to update its wallpaper status.

Wallpaper ownership and the ready state

LiveStagix checks which screen destinations are currently using its wallpaper. The app's compact resting view is associated with both Home and Lock being owned by LiveStagix; owning only one screen does not mean both destinations are set. When you select a new media item, return to the controls and follow the available Set as Wallpaper action again.

Android manufacturers can customise the wallpaper confirmation flow. The exact wording and whether Home, Lock or Both is offered can vary by device and Android version.

On some devices, replacing an existing LiveStagix wallpaper may also remove the current Lock screen wallpaper. If that happens, set your preferred Lock screen wallpaper again from your phone's wallpaper settings.

07 / Your library

Prepared Media & Local Backup

Open Prepared Media from Controls to find and manage wallpaper versions that have already been prepared. Use the three-dot menu for library and storage actions.

Prepared Media page and menu

The Prepared Media page shows storage information and the Local backup and Hide from Gallery switches. The Choose folder or Change folder button selects the shared-storage destination. The three-dot menu contains Restore and Clear all.

  • Restore: checks the selected backup location, restores verified prepared copies that are missing or fail integrity checks, and can rebuild missing library entries from healthy files already stored privately. It also checks for a valid configuration backup.
  • Clear all: after confirmation, removes LiveStagix-managed prepared-media archives, user-configuration backups and diagnostic-log backups, plus non-active prepared-media files and diagnostic logs from private app storage. It keeps the active wallpaper, current app settings and selected Local backup folder. Unrelated files in the selected folder are left untouched. Cleanup failures are reported; affected items or copies are kept where possible so the operation can be retried.

Prepared media has a 200 MB soft storage limit. When space needs to be reclaimed, LiveStagix uses least-recently-used cleanup for eligible items. The active wallpaper and media currently being prepared are protected. Associated shared backups are cleaned up with the media; if safe backup cleanup fails, the app preserves the remaining copy and reports the problem rather than treating it as deleted.

Choose a backup folder

On the Prepared Media page, turn on Local backup and tap Choose folder. Use Android's folder picker to select a location you control, such as an eligible folder in Documents, Pictures or Videos. LiveStagix creates a LiveWP/ folder there and copies your configuration and retained prepared media. Completed Event Recorder sessions are copied to LiveWP/Logs/ when you turn the recorder off while Local backup is enabled. Keep the selected folder accessible when you want to restore from it.

When Local backup is off, LiveStagix keeps its working data in private app storage. Turning Local backup off stops future shared-storage reads and writes; backup files already created in the selected folder are left untouched. Restore remains in the three-dot menu and checks the selected backup location when you tap it. Use Change folder to select a different destination; copies in the previous location are not automatically removed.

Hide from Gallery

The optional Hide from Gallery setting controls whether Android's media scanner indexes supported media in the app-managed LiveWP/ folder. It is off by default. When you turn it on, LiveStagix creates an empty .nomedia marker in that folder. Gallery apps that follow Android's media index will normally stop showing media from the folder and its subfolders. Existing files are not deleted, moved or changed.

This setting is available when a supported, accessible LiveWP/ destination is selected. It is independent of Local backup and can be changed while Local backup is either on or off. Turning Local backup off does not remove the marker or change this setting. Turn Hide from Gallery off to remove the marker and allow Android to index the folder again; the Gallery may take time to refresh.

What this does not do: .nomedia is not a security feature. It does not prevent other apps with storage access from reading the files or stop a cloud or storage provider from syncing them. Apps that scan storage independently may also behave differently.
A backup is useful only when its folder remains available. Avoid deleting or moving the LiveStagix backup folder if you may need to restore from it later.

08 / Preferences

Advanced Settings

Open Advanced Settings from the app's settings control to manage app preferences, rendering and diagnostics. Some options are device- or distribution-dependent.

Theme and renderer

  • Theme: choose the available System, Dark or Light appearance.
  • Renderer: Auto lets the app choose a suitable backend; Modern uses the modern rendering path; Compatibility selects the compatibility path when supported. The active backend can differ from the requested setting when a backend is unavailable.

Updates

LiveStagix checks for updates when the app opens and when it returns to the foreground. You can also check manually from Advanced Settings. If you choose Not now for an available update, that version's popup is dismissed while a reminder shortcut remains available in the app.

Diagnostics and privacy

The optional Event Recorder is available in Advanced Settings and should be enabled only when needed for troubleshooting. It records diagnostic sessions locally. When you turn it off, the session is finalized and, if Local backup is enabled, one completed log file is copied to LiveWP/Logs/. The Clear Logs control attempts to remove private sessions and exported log files from the selected backup folder. Runtime Trace and Motion Diagnostics are separate optional diagnostic controls. Read the Privacy Policy to understand how app data is handled. The Security & Integrity page explains release verification information.

09 / Quick help

Troubleshooting

Why are Motion controls unavailable?

Photo Motion is designed for photo wallpapers. Select a photo to use 2D, 3D or Rotation controls. Video uses the Enhancements panel instead.

Why are Video Enhancements unavailable?

Enhancements are for video playback. Select a video to access Frame Interpolation and Smart Upscale options.

Why is Smart Upscale enabled but not active?

Enabled is the selected preference; active means the current video and device conditions allow that processing path to run. Try Auto or a lighter quality setting if playback performance is affected.

Why can't I adjust Blur or Brightness in Crop & Frame?

Those background adjustments require the media to be zoomed out and Background to be set to Blur. Feather is also intended for the zoomed-out background blend.

Does tuning change my original photo or video?

No. Media Tuner works on prepared media and creates a separate Tuned prepared version when you apply the changes.

What if a prepared item is missing?

Open Prepared Media, choose Restore from its three-dot menu and let LiveStagix check the configured backup location and repair eligible library entries.

Why does the wallpaper look or behave differently on another phone?

Android wallpaper behaviour, GPU capability, refresh rate, codecs and manufacturer background restrictions vary. A setting that works on one phone may use a different processing path or be unavailable on another.
