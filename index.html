07 / QUICK ANSWERS

A few things
worth knowing.

Clear answers before you install.

What media can I use?

LiveStagix supports photo and video wallpapers selected from your device. Web Wallpaper Search lets you find portrait photos online. Photo and video wallpapers use different controls and processing paths.

Does LiveStagix need an internet connection?

Local media playback, gyro motion and on-device rendering do not need a cloud AI service. Web Wallpaper Search and online downloads do require an internet connection.

Do Smart Upscale and Frame Interpolation always run?

No. Their actual runtime activity depends on the source media, display refresh rate, output size and supported processing path. A setting can remain enabled while a feature is ineligible to process a particular video.

Will it work the same on every Android phone?

Not necessarily. Live wallpaper behavior, GPU support and manufacturer-specific background restrictions vary. The app includes adaptive and compatibility paths, but hardware and system limitations still apply.

Can I tune a prepared photo or video?

Yes. Open Media Tuner in Live Stage for a prepared photo or video. Use Light, Color and Details controls, and press and hold Before to compare the unadjusted preview. Applying your edits creates a separate prepared copy.

Does tuning immediately change my active wallpaper?

No. Apply creates a tuned prepared version; you still choose when to set that version as your wallpaper. The tuner does not automatically replace the wallpaper already active in Android.

What happens if an active wallpaper update fails?

LiveStagix attempts an in-place update up to three times. If those attempts fail, it falls back to the wallpaper replacement flow and opens Android's wallpaper preview and confirmation.

How do I check for app updates?

Open Advanced Settings to check manually. LiveStagix also checks for updates when the app is opened, at most once every 24 hours; it does not need a separate daily background service.

Is there anything to know when changing a wallpaper?

When replacing an existing LiveStagix wallpaper, your current Lock screen wallpaper may also be removed during the replacement process. If this happens, you may need to set your preferred Lock screen wallpaper again in your phone's wallpaper settings. This behaviour may vary by device and system software.

How do I install the APK?

Open the official release page, download the APK asset, open it on your Android device and follow Android's installation prompts. Only download releases from the official repository linked on this page.

Where do I find the latest version?

The download buttons open the latest-release endpoint in the public LiveWP-Releases repository. The release page is the source of truth for the APK and its notes.
