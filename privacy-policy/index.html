YOUR DATA, YOUR DEVICE

Privacy Policy.

This policy explains how the LiveStagix Android app handles your media and settings, and how the LiveStagix website uses Microsoft Clarity analytics. The app keeps its working data on your device. Local Backup and Event Recorder are optional, and LiveStagix does not operate a cloud backup service or an app account service. Some features connect to third parties, such as Bing and Google Play, as explained below.

Last updated: November 9, 2026Applies to: LiveStagix website and Android app

1. About LiveStagix

LiveStagix is an independently developed Android live-wallpaper app and project. For privacy questions, contact livestagix.contact@gmail.com.

2. Information handled by the app

LiveStagix processes the media and settings you choose, along with technical information needed to run the app:

  • Photos and videos: When you select media through Android's picker or another file source, the app may copy it into private app storage while preparing, tuning or displaying your wallpaper.
  • Prepared media: The app can create resized, cropped or tuned versions, thumbnails and technical media information. Prepared photos and MP4 videos may contain a LiveStagix marker with app-generated identifiers, media type and crop/background settings. The marker is not encrypted and is not proof that a file was created by LiveStagix.
  • Settings and device details: Wallpaper choices, appearance, rendering and motion settings are stored on your device. The app may also read technical details such as display size and refresh rate, Android version, and device or graphics capabilities to choose suitable settings.
  • In-app background media: This feature is off by default on a fresh installation. You can enable it by turning off Disable Background Media in Advanced Settings. It creates a small, blurred companion image from the prepared wallpaper. For video wallpapers, it uses a representative still frame, not a second background video. The companion stays in app-private no-backup storage and is not uploaded to a LiveStagix server.

These are app operations on your device. LiveStagix does not upload your selected photos, videos, settings or working files to a server operated by LiveStagix.

3. Optional backups and diagnostics

Local Backup is off by default. When you enable it and choose a folder through Android's folder picker, the app can save a configuration file, validated prepared-media backups and completed diagnostic logs in a LiveWP/ folder at that location. The app does not send these files to a LiveStagix server. Android and the selected storage provider control which folders are available; that provider's own privacy practices may also apply.

Hide from Gallery is a separate optional setting and is off by default. When enabled, it creates an empty .nomedia marker in an accessible app-managed LiveWP/ folder. Android media scanners that respect the marker normally skip supported media in that folder and its subfolders. Turning the setting off removes the marker, not the media. It does not block other apps that already have storage access or stop a storage provider from syncing files. This setting can work while Local Backup is off, as long as the folder is accessible.

Event Recorder is off by default in both app editions. When enabled, it records technical diagnostics such as app and wallpaper events, errors, stack traces, timestamps, and device or runtime details. Separate runtime-trace or gyro-diagnostics options can add rendering or motion information. Recordings stay in private app storage. Turning the recorder off finalizes the session; if Local Backup is enabled and its folder is available, the completed session is exported as one log file in LiveWP/Logs/.

Event Recorder does not automatically upload logs to LiveStagix. Review a log before you copy or share it, because its technical details may be visible to the recipient. Clear Logs attempts to remove private sessions and exported logs, but Android or the storage provider may prevent some files from being removed. Turning Local Backup off stops further backup activity but does not delete existing files.

4. Third-party services and website analytics

Web wallpaper search: If you use this feature, LiveStagix opens Bing Images in an embedded browser and sends your search terms to Bing. Microsoft may also receive information such as your IP address, browser details and cookies. When you preview or download an image, your device connects to the website hosting it. Opening an image source or external link also shares the usual connection information with that website. Those services handle information under their own privacy policies.

Google Play: The Play edition may use Google Play to check for and install app updates. Google handles those interactions under its own privacy terms. Android, Google Play and your device manufacturer may also process installation or system diagnostics independently of LiveStagix.

GitHub updates: GitHub-distributed builds connect to GitHub's public Releases API to check for available app updates. GitHub may receive standard connection information, including your IP address and technical request metadata. Choosing Update now downloads the APK and its SHA-256 checksum from the official public GitHub release repository. Before saving the APK, LiveStagix verifies it against the checksum, confirms that it belongs to the LiveStagix package and checks that its version is not older than the installed app. On Android 10 and newer, the verified APK is saved to the public Downloads collection. On older supported Android versions, Android's system file picker asks you to choose where to save it. LiveStagix then opens the Downloads screen when available; otherwise, you can open the Files app yourself. You tap the saved APK in Downloads or Files to start the installation. Android may ask you to allow app installations from the Files or Downloads app. That permission is managed by Android for the app you use to open the APK, not by LiveStagix. Android presents the installation for your confirmation; LiveStagix does not install updates silently.

Website analytics: The LiveStagix website uses Microsoft Clarity to understand how visitors use its pages. Clarity may collect page and interaction information, such as clicks, scrolling and mouse movements, along with browser and device details. It may use cookies or similar technologies to recognise browsers and group visits into sessions. The website does not currently provide a separate LiveStagix switch for Clarity. Microsoft documents its own privacy controls and automatic masking.

Website analytics are separate from the Android app. Clarity does not access wallpaper files, app backups or diagnostic logs stored on your device. Read the Microsoft Privacy Statement and Microsoft Clarity Terms of Use for Microsoft's practices and controls.

5. Storage, retention and deletion

App-private files and optional Local Backup copies are separate. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage normally removes private app data and its saved folder selection, but does not automatically delete backup files in a folder you chose. After reinstalling, you may need to select that folder again to restore available backups.

Turning Local Backup off stops further backup reads and writes, but leaves existing copies untouched. Choosing a different folder also does not delete copies in the previous folder. Restoring settings or prepared media does not delete the backup copy. If a .nomedia marker is present, it remains until Hide from Gallery is turned off or you remove the marker yourself.

Deleting an individual prepared item makes the app attempt to remove its shared backup only when no other private copy still uses it. The confirmed Clear all action in Prepared Media removes LiveStagix-managed prepared-media, configuration and diagnostic backups, along with non-active prepared files and diagnostic logs in private storage. It keeps the active wallpaper, current settings and selected backup folder, and leaves unrelated files in that folder untouched. The app reports cleanup failures where possible.

To delete other backup files, use your device's file manager or the selected storage provider. A provider may have its own syncing and deletion rules. Local Backup files are not encrypted by LiveStagix. Anyone or any app with sufficient access to the selected location may be able to read, copy, change or delete them, so avoid storing sensitive content in shared backups.

LiveStagix does not maintain an online user profile or account, so it has no LiveStagix account data to retrieve or delete. Information held by Microsoft, Google or an image-hosting website must be managed through that provider's own privacy controls.

6. Permissions and security

Android permissions and system access support wallpaper setup, media you choose to import, internet requests for web features, notifications where required for ongoing services, and the optional floating overlay. For Local Backup and Hide from Gallery, the app uses access granted to the folder you select through Android's folder picker. Android and the storage provider determine which folders can be accessed. You can review app permissions in Android Settings and change the selected folder in the app.

GitHub-distributed APK builds do not request Android's REQUEST_INSTALL_PACKAGES permission. For GitHub updates, LiveStagix verifies the downloaded APK and saves it to Downloads, or uses Android's system file picker on older supported versions. You open the saved APK through the Files or Downloads app to start installation. Android may ask you to allow installations from that file manager; this source-specific setting is managed by Android and does not grant LiveStagix permission to install packages. Android presents the installation for your confirmation. Separately, when you first install an APK downloaded from the LiveStagix website, your browser may need Android's permission to install apps from that browser. The Google Play build uses Google Play's update flow instead.

Active app data is kept in app-managed private storage. Backup copies are stored in the location you choose and are not encrypted by LiveStagix. The prepared-media marker can be inspected or forged; it is used to help recognise files, not to secure them or prove who created them. No storage or network method is guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Children, policy updates and contact

The LiveStagix website and app are general-purpose products and are not specifically directed at children under 18. The app does not ask you to create a LiveStagix account or provide personal profile details to the project.

This policy may change when app features or data practices change. The latest version will be published at this URL, with its update date shown near the top.

For questions about this policy or how LiveStagix handles information, email livestagix.contact@gmail.com.
